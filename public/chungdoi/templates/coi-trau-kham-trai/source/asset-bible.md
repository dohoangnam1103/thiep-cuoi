## Concept

The set imagines a contemporary Vietnamese trau cau caddy as a quiet wedding invitation frame. The cover and interior use the same soft octagon so they can behave like a closed and opened lacquer object. Betel leaves and areca blossoms are intentionally sparse, asymmetrical, and ceremonial rather than decorative filler.

## Visual Rules

Use only the defined six-color palette. Lacquer plum carries the main body, betel green carries leaf mass, warm ivory provides blank insert zones and blossoms, shell pearl and aged silver form the inlay language, and restrained coral is reserved for areca fruit accents.

All artwork is vector-only SVG. The transparent canvas outside octagonal or isolated motif silhouettes is part of the design system and should remain untouched. The central medallion and tray insert are intentionally blank for runtime invitation content.

## Lighting Assumption

Lighting is soft satin light from the upper left. Gradients are limited to subtle lacquer depth and restrained shell shifts, avoiding chrome, neon, and high-gloss effects.

## Mobile Readability

Primary silhouettes are broad: octagonal caddy forms, compartment wells, vine stems, leaves, blossoms, and fruit remain legible at small sizes. Fine inlay strokes are used as accents only and never as the sole readable structure.

## Forbidden Motifs

Do not add dragons, phoenixes, lotus, generic Chinese motifs, text, watermarks, signatures, trademarks, logos, raster images, downloaded artwork, embedded image data, or expensive SVG filters.
