# Home V11 editorial journey and live invitation studio

Home V11 adds a no-indexed Vietnamese lab route with a layered hero, GSAP choreography, a live name form, and a sticky phone walkthrough. The `.hp-v11` scope prevents direct styling regressions to production Home2, but the mobile phone choreography does not stick and the reveal system can remove the real form and other core content from the accessibility tree. Lab-only translations also reach every public localized page, reduced-motion detection arrives after GSAP initialization, and responsive/form lifecycle gaps remain.

Watch for: the broken mobile sticky journey (**confirmed**), inaccessible `autoAlpha` reveals (**confirmed**), and app-wide V11 catalog payload (**confirmed**).

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

The V11 route and registry resolve through the Vietnamese-only routing setup, but the client translation boundary is global. The root `NextIntlClientProvider` inherits the complete request catalog, so the new 7.7 KB V11 namespace is embedded in unrelated pages such as `/` and `/bang-gia` (**confirmed**).

Two motion choices break fallbacks: mobile changes `overflow: clip` to `overflow: hidden`, making the guest section the sticky container, while `autoAlpha: 0` keeps unrevealed semantic content `visibility:hidden` until visual scrolling trips each trigger (**confirmed**). Reduced motion is read in a passive effect after `useGSAP` runs in a layout effect (**confirmed**).

The phone has no viewport-height cap, so 720px desktop windows and landscape phones clip its bottom while sticky (**confirmed**). The form’s displayed fallback names differ from its submitted values, and its ARIA setup combines repeated typing announcements with an ignored illustration label (**confirmed**).

Offscreen decorative loops remain active for the route lifetime (**likely**), roughly 6.2 MB of public source/reference images have no runtime consumer (**confirmed**), and no V11 test covers the route, motion preference, form defaults, or responsive breakpoints (**confirmed**).

<summary>Issues (10)</summary>

1. **[High, confirmed] Mobile sticky containment** — the mobile `overflow: hidden` ancestor makes the phone scroll away instead of staying beside the four guest stages. Retain `overflow: clip` or isolate clipping in a non-ancestor wrapper.
2. **[High, confirmed] Inaccessible reveal state** — `autoAlpha: 0` applies `visibility:hidden` to headings, CTAs, and the real form before visual intersection. Animate opacity/transform without hiding semantic content.
3. **[Medium, confirmed] Global V11 catalog payload** — the 7.7 KB lab namespace is serialized into unrelated localized pages. Filter lab namespaces from the root client provider and inject V11 messages only at its route.
4. **[Medium, confirmed] Reduced-motion startup race** — GSAP’s layout effect runs once with `reducedMotion=false` before the passive media-query effect updates it. Guard synchronously with `matchMedia` before constructing any timeline.
5. **[Medium, confirmed] Short-viewport phone clipping** — the width-derived phone exceeds available height at 1280×720 and landscape mobile. Add an `svh`-derived width cap and short-height checks.
6. **[Medium, confirmed] Preview/submission name mismatch** — the card displays fallback names while the form persists empty strings. Initialize or submit the displayed defaults, or remove the fallback/prefill promise.
7. **[Low, confirmed] Preview ARIA contract** — the full card announces on each keystroke, while the generic phone `div` ignores its `aria-label`. Remove/debounce the live region and use a naming-capable group or caption.
8. **[Low, likely] Offscreen animation lifetime** — infinite GSAP/CSS decoration runs before its sections are visible. Pause those loops outside their section intersections.
9. **[Low, confirmed] Unused public asset payload** — about 6.2 MB of source PNGs/contact sheets has no runtime consumer but is deployed with `public`. Move it outside `public` or omit it.
10. **[Low, confirmed] Missing V11 regression tests** — no automated check covers routing, sticky behavior, viewport fit, reduced motion, or form defaults. Add a focused responsive Playwright spec.

<summary>Details</summary>

### High — Mobile guest journey never sticks (**confirmed**)

The base guest section uses `overflow: clip` at `src/components/home2/lab/v11.css:932-939`, but the mobile override changes it to `overflow: hidden` at `v11.css:1723-1725`, directly above the sticky phone rule at `v11.css:1727-1731`. That overflow ancestor becomes the sticky containing mechanism, so the phone moves with the section instead of staying at `top: 4.25rem` while the four steps pass it.

At 390×844, Chromium measured the phone at viewport top `100px`, then `-200px`, `-700px`, and `-1300px` as the steps scrolled. Keep `overflow: clip` on mobile, or clip only a separate decorative wrapper, so no ancestor between the phone and viewport establishes an overflow scroll container.

### High — Scroll reveals hide semantic content from assistive technology (**confirmed**)

Every `[data-v11-reveal]` element starts from `autoAlpha: 0` in `src/components/home2/lab/v11-motion.ts:113-125`. GSAP maps that to zero opacity plus `visibility: hidden`, removing content from the accessibility tree until ScrollTrigger observes visual scrolling. This includes the story proof (`v11-page.tsx:276-288`), the complete invitation form (`v11-page.tsx:368-405`), capability copy (`v11-page.tsx:435-454`), guest heading (`v11-page.tsx:479-486`), and finale CTA (`v11-page.tsx:649-670`). A screen-reader virtual cursor does not reliably cross visual trigger thresholds, and keyboard focus cannot enter a `visibility:hidden` form.

Animate opacity and transform without changing visibility, or animate a decorative child while keeping headings, controls, and links exposed to assistive technology.

### Medium — Lab-only V11 messages are sent on every public route (**confirmed**)

`src/i18n/request.ts:5-13` returns the complete Vietnamese catalog, and `src/app/[locale]/layout.tsx:140` mounts `NextIntlClientProvider` without a narrowed `messages` prop. Adding `homeLabV11` at `messages/vi.json:1507` therefore adds 7,713 raw JSON bytes before RSC serialization and compression to every client provider under `[locale]`. Current HTML for `/` and `/bang-gia` contains V11-only strings including “Contemporary Wedding Editorial” and “Không chỉ được gửi”.

Exclude the lab namespaces from the root client message object, then provide only `homeLabV11` from a nested provider in `src/app/[locale]/home-2/lab/v11/page.tsx:36-42`. Server-side lab-index translations can keep using the full request catalog.

### Medium — Reduced-motion users receive the full GSAP startup pass first (**confirmed**)

`useReducedMotion` starts at `false` and reads `matchMedia` in `useEffect` at `src/components/home2/lab/v11-motion.ts:11-20`. `useV11Motion` passes that initial value into `useGSAP` at `v11-motion.ts:25-39`; the installed hook runs in a layout effect, while preference synchronization is passive. On an initial reduced-motion visit, the intro can install transforms, opacity, visibility, and filter animation for a paint before state flips and the context reverts. The CSS query at `v11.css:1816-1847` cannot prevent that first JavaScript pass.

Check `window.matchMedia("(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)").matches` synchronously at the start of the `useGSAP` callback before creating timelines, while retaining the subscription for live preference changes.

### Medium — The sticky phone is taller than short viewports (**confirmed**)

The phone is width-capped with `aspect-ratio: 0.51` at `src/components/home2/lab/v11.css:1005-1009`; sticky offsets are fixed at `6rem` and `4.25rem` (`v11.css:1591-1594`, `1727-1731`). At 1280×720, the wrapper is 658px tall with 624px available below the offset. At 844×390, it is 526px tall with 322px available. The screen clips overflow at `v11.css:1029-1033`, leaving its bottom RSVP confirmation unreachable.

Cap phone width from available `svh` as well as horizontal space—for example, `(100svh - sticky offset - safety gap) * 0.51`—and validate 1280×720 plus landscape mobile.

### Medium — Preview fallback names are not persisted (**confirmed**)

The fields initialize empty at `src/components/home2/lab/v11-page.tsx:56-57`, while the preview substitutes translated `Minh`/`Hà` values at `v11-page.tsx:101-102`. The controls submit the empty state at `v11-page.tsx:376-398`, despite the prefill promise at `messages/vi.json:1566`. `createInvitation` persists those empty values at `src/app/dashboard/actions.ts:31-54`, so an untouched submission opens an editor that does not match the preview.

Initialize the controlled values to the displayed defaults or explicitly submit the fallbacks. If blanks are intentional, remove the visual fallback and change the catalog promise.

### Low — Live preview ARIA is noisy and the phone label is ignored (**confirmed**)

The complete card is `aria-live="polite"` at `src/components/home2/lab/v11-page.tsx:351`, so each typed character can repeatedly announce card content instead of leaving the labeled inputs to speak normally. Remove the live region or replace it with a concise, debounced completion status.

The phone applies `aria-label` to a generic `div` at `v11-page.tsx:496-503`. Chromium’s ARIA snapshot exposes only panel text, not `guest.phoneAriaLabel`. Use a naming-capable `group` role or a `figure` with a hidden caption while preserving active-panel descendants.

### Low — Decorative animation runs for the full route lifetime (**likely**)

The paper tween starts on mount with `repeat: -1` and no ScrollTrigger at `src/components/home2/lab/v11-motion.ts:157-165`, several screens before its section. Four reply cards also start infinite CSS animations at `src/components/home2/lab/v11.css:1511-1548` while the finale is offscreen. Route cleanup removes the GSAP tween, but during the visit its ticker still updates an offscreen transform every frame.

Create the paper tween paused and play/pause it from a section-level ScrollTrigger; pause or gate reply-card animation until the finale intersects.

### Low — Public V11 assets include 6.2 MB of unused source/reference files (**confirmed**)

Runtime backgrounds use the WebP files at `src/components/home2/lab/v11.css:195` and `1609`; no component or stylesheet references `hero-editorial-stage.png`, either full-size background PNG, or the two contact sheets. They account for about 6.2 MB of the 15 MB V11 public directory. Production copies or bind-mounts all of `public` (`Dockerfile:118`; `scripts/deploy-fast.sh:458`), increasing deploy synchronization and disk use without browser requests.

Move generation sources/contact sheets outside `public` or omit them, retaining only runtime assets.

### Low — No V11 regression coverage protects these breakpoints (**confirmed**)

No `src/**/*.test.ts` or `tests/**/*.spec.ts` references `/home-2/lab/v11`, `V11Page`, `useV11Motion`, or the guest journey. Add a focused Playwright spec covering the lab-index link, sticky phone at 390×844, phone fit at 1280×720 and landscape mobile, reduced motion set before load, and equality between previewed and submitted names.

Targeted ESLint for the four changed TS/TSX files and `npm run typecheck` pass. The route returns HTTP 200 through the Vietnamese rewrite. Full `npm run lint` exceeded the 180-second review budget, so targeted ESLint was used for changed code.
