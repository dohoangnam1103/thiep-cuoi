# Long Phụng Gatefold — concept prompts

These prompts are for visual-direction previews only. They do not request
transparent production layers, material masks or generated typography.

Shared constraints:

- One premium vertical gatefold wedding invitation shown as a physical object.
- Closed front view and partially opened three-quarter view in the same board.
- Two half-width hinged wings meeting at a real center seam.
- Dragon composition belongs to the left wing; phoenix belongs to the right.
- Deep lacquer crimson, antique gold, warm ivory and lacquer black.
- Clear central safe area for DOM-rendered couple names and date.
- Visible thickness, hinge logic, inner paper, back-face hint and contact shadow.
- No text, letters, Chinese characters, logos, signatures or watermark.
- No human figures, venue scene or unrelated props.
- No generic fantasy anatomy.
- Do not merge the whole design into a flat poster.
