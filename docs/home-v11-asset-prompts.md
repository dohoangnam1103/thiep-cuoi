# Home V11 — Generated Asset Brief

## Shared art direction

**Concept:** Contemporary Vietnamese Wedding Editorial.

Visual balance: 50% soft editorial, 30% paper architecture, 20% editorial monochrome. The result should feel like a premium fashion magazine and a contemporary gallery installation, not a traditional red-and-gold wedding set and not a SaaS landing page.

**Palette**
- Warm ivory `#F5F2EC`
- Ink black `#171716`
- Soft stone gray `#D8D3CA`
- Dusty rose `#D9BABC`
- Wine-red accent `#A52C46`

**Materials and light**
- Tactile cotton invitation paper with deckled or precisely cut edges
- Translucent organza/voile rather than heavy satin
- Soft architectural daylight plus one crisp editorial highlight
- Restrained shadows, physical depth, subtle paper grain
- Photographic realism with art-directed editorial composition

**Vietnamese identity**
- Expressed through Vietnamese people, family/wedding context, and culturally grounded invitation rituals; wardrobe may use refined contemporary international wedding fashion
- No requirement for traditional ornaments or traditional Vietnamese clothing
- Avoid imagery that reads primarily Chinese, Japanese, Korean, or as a Western church interior; European bridal attire itself is intentional

**Global negative constraints**
- No readable text, fake lettering, logos, signatures, or watermarks
- No bright red-and-gold palette, lacquer surfaces, liquid glass, neon gradients, plastic 3D-render look, hearts, confetti, champagne, diamond-ring close-ups, church architecture, lanterns, dragons, phoenixes, or dense floral borders
- No excessive props; every composition needs generous negative space for live UI and kinetic typography
- No malformed hands, duplicate people, distorted invitation edges, or illegible pseudo-type
